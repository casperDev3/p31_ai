Behind the crate

Four steps. Zero detours.

From the moment an apple leaves the branch, it's on a cold-chain stopwatch.

1

Hand-picked

Pickers walk the rows daily during peak window — only fruit at the right sugar level comes down.

2

Sorted on-site

Optical sizing and a manual second pass. Bruised or undersized fruit becomes cider, not waste.

3

Cold-packed

Pre-cooled to 2°C within two hours, packed into ventilated crates lined with recycled pulp.

4

Direct-shipped

Refrigerated truck or local courier. No warehouse middlemen, no shelf time.
